Real Capabilities

What SS TV Actually Delivers

Every feature below is built directly into the app—tailored separately for touchscreens on mobile and D-pad remotes on Android TV.

01

Adaptive Android TV & Mobile Interfaces

One APK automatically detects your device. On phones and tablets you get bottom tab navigation, a Home featured most-watched banner, Continue Watching, and multi-column touch grids. On Android TV, SS TV switches to a left sidebar rail with remembered D-pad focus and disables heavy animations and gradients so even older TV boxes stay fast and responsive.

Auto TV Detection Zero-Animation TV Mode Continue Watching
02

Smart Stream Auto-Failover & Auto-Reconnect

Powered by AndroidX Media3 ExoPlayer for HLS and DASH live streams. If a broadcast source stalls or drops, SS TV automatically rolls over to backup stream URLs within your chosen failover timeout (5s, 10s, 15s, or 20s) and automatically resumes live playback when your internet connection returns.

HLS & DASH 5s–20s Failover Offline Reconnect
03

Quality Picker, Aspect Ratio & Decoder Engine

Switch between Auto or specific stream resolutions whenever multi-bitrate variants exist. Fine-tune playback with Fit / Fill / Zoom scaling, three buffer presets (Fast Zapping, Balanced, High Stability Anti-Freeze), Hardware or Software decoder modes, and one-tap stream Reload right inside the player.

Quality Selector Fit / Fill / Zoom HW / SW Decoder
04

Mobile Gestures, Screen Lock & Picture-in-Picture

Watch in portrait while browsing more channels underneath, or toggle fullscreen landscape without reloading the stream. Swipe left or right to zap channels, slide the left edge for independent video brightness or the right edge for volume, lock screen touches, or pop the stream into floating Picture-in-Picture (Android 8.0+).

PiP Mode Swipe Zap / Vol / Bright Screen Lock
05

TV Cinema Control HUD & In-Player Browse Shelf

While watching on TV, press OK to open the bottom control bar with Favorite, Stream Quality, Aspect Ratio, Channels, and Reload buttons. Press Down to bring up the categorized Channel Browse Shelf over live video, or type digits 0–9 on your remote to jump directly to any channel number.

0–9 Number Tune Categorized Shelf CH+ / CH− Zap
06

Favorites, Custom Arrange & Startup Behavior

Long-press any channel card (or hold OK on TV) to save it to Favorites without interrupting browsing. Reorder your Favorite channels using drag-and-drop on mobile or D-pad grab/move/drop on TV. Enable Auto-Play Last Channel, Open Favorites on Startup, or per-category channel numbering from 1.

Custom Favorite Order Auto-Play Last Category Numbering
07

Live Text Search & Voice Search

Find channels instantly by name or category from the dedicated Search tab, or trigger the built-in Voice Search overlay to speak a channel name and jump straight to the match without typing on a TV remote.

Instant Filter Voice Recognition Category Match
08

11 Themes, Private PIN Channels & In-App Updates

Personalize SS TV with 11 dark-room color palettes—including Crimson Velvet, Electric Cyan, Liquid Glass, Royal Amethyst, and Emerald Pulse. Optionally unlock PIN-protected private channels with a 4-digit keypad, and receive official app updates directly inside the app with live download progress.

11 Color Themes 4-Digit Private PIN In-App OTA Updater
